# ReClaim Brand Guide

**Real recovery. Lasting impact.**

The ReClaim identity combines three ideas: the **R** for ReClaim, a **leaf** for material recovery and healthier communities, and an open **circular recovery ring** for accountable movement from claim to verified recovery.

## Core palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#0B2E1B` | Primary dark surface, headings, icon background |
| Growth | `#63B832` | Primary green accent |
| Leaf light | `#B7EA54` | Highlight and gradient start |
| Carbon | `#1F2937` | Body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Light surface and reversed mark |

The green gradient runs from Leaf light to Growth. Do not use color alone to communicate claim, verification, or reward state.

## Typography

Use **Inter** where available, with a system sans-serif fallback. Favor strong, compact headings and highly legible body copy. Brand assets do not require the Inter font file to be stored in the repository.

## Logo assets

- `assets/brand/reclaim-mark.svg` — primary square symbol.
- `assets/brand/reclaim-wordmark.svg` — horizontal mark with tagline.
- `assets/brand/reclaim-icon-light.svg` — rounded light app/icon treatment.
- `assets/brand/reclaim-icon-dark.svg` — rounded dark app/icon treatment.
- `assets/brand/favicon.svg` — simplified small-size mark.

Keep clear space around the mark roughly equal to the width of the R's vertical stem. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual pieces arbitrarily, add unapproved effects, or place the detailed wordmark at sizes where the tagline becomes illegible.

## Voice

ReClaim should sound precise, constructive, and accountable. Prefer concrete terms such as **pending claim**, **physical verification**, **recovery receipt**, and **verified reward** over vague sustainability claims.

Never imply that scanning equals recycling or that AI establishes physical recovery. The brand promise depends on the same evidence boundaries as the product:

> scan ≠ recovery  
> AI observation ≠ verification  
> pending value ≠ verified reward

## Tagline

Primary: **Real recovery. Lasting impact.**

Supporting language may emphasize cleaner communities, stronger local recovery systems, transparent evaluation, and accountable technology, but environmental or economic impact claims should be tied to measured evidence rather than assumed from participation.

## Accessibility

Maintain strong text/background contrast. Provide text alternatives for meaningful brand images. At small sizes use the favicon or icon mark rather than shrinking the full horizontal wordmark. Never rely on green/red alone for lifecycle status.
