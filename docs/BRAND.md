# ReClaim Brand Direction

## Name

**ReClaim**

## Core idea

ReClaim is a verified recovery network. It connects a container's identity, a pending recovery claim, physical facility evidence, and an auditable recovery receipt without treating a barcode scan as proof of recycling.

## Logo direction

The primary mark should communicate **return + verification + circular recovery** without relying on the generic three-arrow recycling symbol alone.

Recommended construction: a clean circular or looping container silhouette whose return path resolves into a subtle check/evidence node. The mark should remain recognizable at app-icon size and in monochrome.

Avoid currency symbols, coins, crypto imagery, government seals, or visual language implying that scanning itself creates money.

## Personality

Trustworthy, modern, practical, evidence-led, local-first, and optimistic. Avoid greenwashing aesthetics and overly futuristic AI imagery.

## Wordmark

Use `ReClaim` with the capital C preserved. The visual emphasis may subtly distinguish `Re` and `Claim`, reinforcing recovery plus verified claim without making either word difficult to read.

## Initial palette direction

Use a restrained recovery palette built around deep evergreen/forest, a brighter glass/teal accent, and warm off-white or neutral backgrounds. The identity must also work in pure black and white.

## Tagline direction

**Verified recovery, from claim to receipt.**

This is positioning language, not a claim that every scan is verified. Product surfaces must continue to distinguish pending observations from physically verified recovery.
